How do I play?

Drag on the board to slide a piece, tap the board to rotate, or use the buttons under the board: arrows to move and nudge down, Hold to save a piece for later, the round arrow to rotate, and Drop to place it instantly.

Where are my scores?

On the Progress screen and on each mode in the Modes list. They are stored only on your device, so deleting the app resets them.

How do I change the look?

Open Themes and tap one. It changes the piece colors, the board, and the glow straight away.

Can I make the buttons smaller, or swap sides?

Yes — open Controls.
