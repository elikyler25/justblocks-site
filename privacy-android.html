Just Blocks for Android

Privacy policy · October 7, 2026

The Android app works offline. It does not send your information or play activity to the developer. It has no advertising, analytics service or required account.

Saved on your device

Your nickname, settings, saved game, scores, achievements and progress are stored on your device. Your nickname is optional and can be changed in Settings.

Android backup

If you enable Android backup or transfer your device, Android may copy the app’s saved data to restore it for you. Google or your device provider handles that service under its own privacy policy. Just Blocks does not receive those backups.

Sharing a result

When you tap Share, Android lets you choose an app or person to receive your result card. The card can include your chosen nickname, score, theme and game board. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination and complete its sharing process. That destination’s privacy policy applies.

Opening support links

Privacy and support links open in your browser. The browser and website may process a normal web request, including an IP address. Gameplay does not make those requests.

Permissions

The game uses haptic feedback for taps and moves. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or shared storage.

Deleting your data

Use Android’s app storage settings to clear local data, or uninstall the app. Manage any device backups separately through your Android backup provider. Data already shared with another app must be managed there.

Children

The Android app does not send personal information to the developer from any player, including children.
