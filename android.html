Just Blocks

Now on Android.

Drop blocks, clear lines and make yourself at home. Play solo or sit opposite a friend for a two-player game on one phone.

Download for Android

Early Android version 1.0 · Android 7 or later · about 133 MB

Install it

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded Just-Blocks-Android-1.0.apk file. If Android asks, allow this browser to install the app. You can turn that permission off afterward.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

This is a direct download from the developer while the Google Play release is being prepared. It includes offline solo games, daily challenges, themes, saved progress and same-phone two-player games. Apple Game Center online matches are available in the iPhone version.

It has no ads, purchases or required account. This early version was checked on an Android emulator; feedback from real phones is welcome.
