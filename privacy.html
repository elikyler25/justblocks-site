Privacy Policy

Just Blocks · Last updated October 2, 2026

The short version

Just Blocks does not collect or sell any personal information. There are no Just Blocks accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your high scores, game statistics, chosen theme, and control settings are saved only on your iPhone so they are there next time you play. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Internet use

Solo games and your progress work completely offline. Just Blocks has no servers of its own.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, Just Blocks uses it for achievements, leaderboards and optional online matches with friends or other players. Apple runs Game Center and manages your Game Center name and connections under Apple’s privacy policy. During an online match, the moves of the match and the character and theme you chose for your player card are sent to the other player. Nothing else is shared.

Sharing

A game result is only shared when you tap Share and choose where to send it.

Children

Because no information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app changes.
