Privacy Policy

Just Blocks · Last updated September 16, 2026

The short version

Just Blocks does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your high scores, game statistics, chosen theme, and control settings are saved only on your iPhone so they are there next time you play. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Internet use

The game runs entirely offline. It does not connect to any server.

Children

Because no information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app changes.
